      Welcome to FiddleCraft

      Build 3D models from simple shapes, then print them. Five things to know:

      1. Add a shape: click one on the right (or drag it onto the blue workplane).
      2. Move it: drag the shape. Drag empty space to turn the view; scroll to zoom.
      3. Change it: drag the black squares to resize, the black cone to lift, the curved arrows to turn, tilt or roll.
      4. Cut holes: make a shape a Hole, select both with Ctrl+click, then press Group.
      5. Print it: press Export for 3D printing to make an STL file for your printer.

      Scribble

      Hold the mouse button down and draw an outline. It becomes a flat 3D shape you can resize and stretch taller.

      Keyboard and mouse

      Tab / Shift+Tab
      Pick the next or previous shape
      Shift+click
      Select several shapes (move, copy, delete or group them together)
      Ctrl+A
      Select every shape
      L
      Align: with two or more shapes selected, dots appear around them. Hover a dot to preview, click it to line the shapes up (edges or middles, left–right, front–back or up–down). Click one of the shapes first to keep it still and line the others up to it. Esc finishes
      Ctrl+Shift+H
      Show every hidden shape again
      Arrow keys
      Move the shape 1 grid step (hold Shift for 10)
      Page Up / Page Down
      Lift or lower the shape
      R / Shift+R
      Turn the shape 15°
      Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V
      Copy, cut and paste the selected shapes
      Ctrl+D
      Duplicate the selected shapes. Move or turn the copy, then press Ctrl+D again to repeat the same step (great for rows and circles)
      Ctrl+G / Ctrl+Shift+G
      Group all shapes (holes are cut out) / ungroup
      Delete
      Remove the shape
      Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y
      Undo / redo
      Ctrl+S / Ctrl+O
      Save the design as a file / open a design file
      H
      Reset the view
      F
      Zoom to fit the selected shapes (or everything)
      O
      Switch between the flat view (no perspective, like a technical drawing) and the normal 3D view
      1 / 2 / 3
      Look from the top, front or side
      Drag the black squares around the base
      Resize the shape (corners change width and depth; hold Shift to keep its proportions)
      Drag the black square on top
      Change the height
      Point at a square, then click a number
      Type an exact size in millimetres. Enter or Tab applies it; Esc keeps the old size
      Drag the black cone
      Lift the shape off the workplane (D drops it back down)
      Drag the curved arrows
      Turn (arrow on the workplane), tilt (arrow on the side) or roll (arrow at the front). A ring appears: on its blue band the shape turns in 22.5° steps; drag further out (or hold Shift) for single degrees
      View cube (top right)
      Click a side, edge or corner to look from there; drag the cube to turn the view
      W (Workplane)
      Click a face of a shape to put a workplane there; new shapes land on it, standing at right angles to the face. Press W and click empty space to go back. Shapes dragged from the panel onto a shape land on the face you drop them on
      Ruler
      Click Ruler, then click the workplane to put it down. Pick a shape to see how far it is from the ruler, and type a number in the orange boxes to move it exactly. The × takes the ruler away
      Drag a shape
      Move it; drag it over another shape and it climbs onto that shape's top, drag it off and it drops back to the workplane
      Drag empty space
      Turn the view, even underneath to look up at the bottom of shapes (right-drag also works; middle-drag pans)
